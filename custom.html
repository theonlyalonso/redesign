Custom Critiques

Add your own lines to the judges' feedback. Each judge gives separate challenge and runway opinions, so they can disagree without changing scores or placements.

The contestant's name is added automatically, or use {queen} to place it yourself. Plain text only; up to 500 characters.

Saved in this browser. Matching custom lines are mixed with built-in feedback, not guaranteed every episode. The franchise host is more supportive; Michelle is stricter, Carson fashion-critical, Ross encouraging, and guests unpredictable.
